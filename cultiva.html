Overview

Cultiva is a unified digital platform connecting farmers, landowners, buyers, and investors to modernize the agricultural ecosystem. It enables direct crop sales, secure land leasing, and sustainable tree-leasing through a transparent, role-based ecosystem that empowers every stakeholder with market-driven insights.

Software Used:
Figma

Context & Backstory

In India, vast agricultural lands remain underutilized due to the lack of secure systems for land leasing, crop sales, and investment. Landowners struggle with legal leasing, while buyers face challenges in sourcing quality produce reliably. Urban investors also lack transparent platforms to explore agricultural opportunities.


Current market solutions are fragmented, focusing on either real estate or crop sales, but failing to unite all stakeholders. This gap creates barriers in trust and transparency, preventing efficient land utilization and hindering the growth of a unified agri-commerce ecosystem.

“Disconnected systems and manual records lead to confusion and lost opportunities” “Fragmented solutions lower trust and financial security in agri-commerce”

Problem
Definition

Land leasing, crop selling, and investment processes lack transparency, trust, and secure digital support. As a result, large portions of land stay underutilized, farmers earn less, and buyers and investors struggle to find reliable opportunities.

Underutilized & Barren Land

Large portions of agricultural land remain unused due to lack of secure, transparent systems for leasing and productive collaboration.

Fragmented Market Ecosystem

Existing platforms only support single functions (real estate, crop sales, investment), creating disconnected experiences for landowners, buyers, and investors.

Lack of Trust & Transparency

Farmers, landowners, and buyers struggle with verifying authenticity, pricing clarity, and reliability—leading to hesitation and missed opportunities.

Inefficient Agri-Commerce Processes

Selling crops, discovering land, or finding investment opportunities requires manual effort, middlemen, and inconsistent communication, slowing growth.

User Research - Summary

  • Many farmers, landowners, buyers, and investors still rely on offline networks, middle man, or word of mouth, leading to unreliable information and inconsistent pricing.
  • Digital platforms exist, but each focuses on only one service—crop selling, land listings, or investment—resulting in fragmented experiences.
  • Users across all roles expressed a strong need for a trusted, transparent, and unified platform that connects all agricultural stakeholders in one place.

User Research - Pain Points

01

Transparency Gaps

Farmers and landowners struggle to verify authenticity or credibility, increasing fraud risks and disputes.

02

Access Barriers

Difficulty finding verified buyers, lessees, or reliable agricultural investment information in real-time.

03

Fragmented Tools

Users switch between multiple platforms for leasing, selling, and pricing, leading to a disconnected experience.

04

Security Risks

Lack of escrow payments, digital contracts, or proper documentation systems to secure large transactions.
