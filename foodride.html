Overview

Food Ride is a streamlined web application/ POS system designed to help restaurant staff manage online orders with clarity and ease. It unifies new, active, and completed orders into one intuitive dashboard, reducing confusion and speeding up workflow. With real-time updates, POS syncing, and simple controls, Food Ride enhances efficiency during busy shifts and supports smoother kitchen-to-delivery operations.

Context & Backstory

The surge in online food delivery has created a fast-paced ecosystem for restaurants, making them heavily reliant on digital platforms to manage incoming orders, kitchen workflows, and delivery partners.


When backend systems are fragmented or poorly designed, staff face high stress and operational errors. This case study focuses on designing a unified, efficient POS workflow to resolve these pain points and streamline everyday kitchen-to-delivery operations.

Staff swap between multiple devices (phone, tablet, POS.) Missing notifications or slow updates cause frustration..

User Research - Summary

  • System Integration Dependency: All interviewed restaurants rely on integrated POS systems (UrbanPiper, POSist, proprietary systems) to manage multiple delivery platforms.
  • Manual-Digital Hybrid Workflows: Despite automation, significant manual intervention remains necessary for status updates and coordination.
  • Multi-Platform Management Challenges: Staff regularly manage 4-6 different interfaces simultaneously.

User Research - Pain Points

1. System Performance Issues

Server lag and crashes during peak hours affect 100% of interviewed locations.

2. Interface Overload

Multiple tab/window management creates cognitive burden and error risk.

3. Limited Access Controls

Staff often lack access to inventory and advanced features, requiring manager escalation.

4. Duplicate Identifier Issues

Same delivery codes causing operational confusion.

Market
Research

Behind every seamless delivery app, restaurant teams juggle multiple complex backend tools to manage orders and kitchen coordination. Understanding these key users and their systems is crucial to identifying operational gaps and improving overall workflow efficiency.

Types of Users Involved

  • → Restaurant Order Coordinators - Manage incoming online orders, verify items, and track order statuses.
  • → Restaurant Supervisors / Managers - Oversee daily operations, resolve delays, and ensure smooth kitchen-to-delivery flow.
  • → Kitchen Staff - Prepare and pack orders efficiently based on real-time updates.
  • → Delivery Partners - Collect orders, confirm pickup codes, and communicate with staff.
  • → Platform Operations Admins - Handle POS syncing, system updates, and technical troubleshooting.

Point of Sale (POS) Systems

POS (Point of Sale) systems integrate with food delivery platforms through direct API connections, middleware, or third-party aggregators, allowing seamless transfer of online orders directly into the restaurant's workflow.

How POS Integration Works?

01

All online orders from multiple delivery platforms are funneled into the POS system automatically, appearing on the same dashboard as dine-in or takeaway orders.

02

The POS syncs menu details (items, prices, availability) in real time with delivery platforms, so updates can be managed centrally.

03

Integrated POS updates inventory and sales data the moment an order is accepted, providing unified analytics and reducing stockouts.

04

Kitchen tickets are generated instantly for orders, minimizing lag and ensuring efficient kitchen workflow.

Scenario

Priya is a full-time restaurant operator who manages the constant flow of online food delivery orders throughout the day. Her role requires juggling multiple delivery apps, coordinating with the kitchen, and updating order statuses while keeping the workflow running smoothly. Switching between different systems and handling peak-hour rush often leaves her feeling pressured and exhausted. She needs a unified, reliable backend solution that helps her manage orders efficiently, reduces confusion, and supports her in delivering a smooth experience from kitchen to doorstep.
